Function in CA2sp: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Mchr1 encodes melanin-concentrating hormone receptor 1, the only functional MCH receptor in rodents. It is a class-A GPCR coupling to Gi/Go and Gq, lowering cAMP and mobilising intracellular calcium in response to the hypothalamic peptide MCH, and it concentrates in the neuronal primary cilium. MCH-MCHR1 signalling regulates feeding, sleep and hippocampus-dependent learning.
Region function
CA2 stratum pyramidale is required for social recognition memory and is a major target of neuromodulatory peptides such as vasopressin and oxytocin that switch its output onto CA1 and lateral septum.
Published in this region
inferred No region-specific literature found. The closest work is in neighbouring CA1, where MCHR1 knockout mice show reduced AMPA and NMDA transmission, impaired LTP and LTD and passive-avoidance deficits (Pachoud 2010; Adamantidis 2005), and Diniz et al. 2020 mapped ciliary MCHR1 across the rodent hippocampus; Allen ISH in CA2sp is low (energy 0.65-0.86) and below CA1sp.
Predicted function
Prediction: ciliary MCHR1 on CA2 pyramidal neurons would act as a slow, state-dependent (sleep, arousal, satiety) modulator biasing CA2 excitability and plasticity rather than driving fast transmission. Intra-CA2 MCH should facilitate, and SNAP-94847 or CA2-restricted Mchr1 deletion should impair, social recognition memory and the NMDA-dependent component of CA2 synaptic drive.
Innovation
★★★★☆ 4/5 MCH signalling in hippocampus has only been examined in CA1, leaving a plausible hypothalamic-to-CA2 state-gating pathway untested despite existing drugs and Cre lines.
Tools
Mchr1 knockout mice (Adamantidis 2005; Zhou 2005); MCHR1-CreER line (Engle 2018); antagonist SNAP-94847 with established in vivo dosing (David 2007); validated anti-MCHR1 antibody for ciliary labelling (Diniz 2020); CA2 access via Amigo2-Cre.
