Function in LC: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
ADGRE1 encodes EMR1, better known in mouse as F4/80. It is an adhesion-class G-protein-coupled receptor with a string of extracellular EGF-like repeats and a GAIN domain. F4/80 is the classic pan-macrophage and microglia surface marker in mouse and is required for the induction of peripheral immune tolerance, while the human orthologue EMR1 is restricted to eosinophils; no ligand or downstream signalling output is firmly established for either.
Region function
The locus coeruleus is the principal noradrenergic nucleus of the brain; its small population of neurons innervates nearly the whole forebrain and sets arousal, attention, stress reactivity, sleep-wake transitions and network gain.
Published in this region
inferred No region-specific literature found. Adgre1/F4/80 in brain labels microglia and perivascular and meningeal macrophages rather than neurons (Hamann et al. 2007), and Wasinski et al. 2023 used hypothalamic Adgre1 as a neuroinflammatory marker; a locus coeruleus signal therefore most plausibly reflects the nucleus's dense perivascular macrophage and microglial population rather than noradrenergic neurons themselves.
Predicted function
Prediction: ADGRE1-positive macrophages and microglia in the locus coeruleus are predicted to act as local immune sentinels whose activation state gates noradrenergic excitability, providing a route by which systemic inflammation produces sickness-associated changes in arousal and attention. Microglia-restricted Adgre1 deletion or macrophage depletion with clodronate or CSF1R inhibitors in LC is predicted to leave baseline sleep-wake architecture intact but to blunt the LPS-induced rise in LC firing and the accompanying sickness-related hypersomnolence and reduced exploratory behaviour; F4/80 immunolabelling combined with Dbh-Cre photometry is the natural first experiment.
Innovation
★★★☆☆ 3/5 Neuroimmune modulation of the locus coeruleus is an interesting and under-explored idea, but because Adgre1 almost certainly marks glia rather than LC neurons the finding is less surprising and the reagents are standard immunology tools.
Tools
F4/80 (Adgre1)-Cre and Adgre1-knockout mice; anti-F4/80 antibody clones BM8 and CI:A3-1; Cx3cr1-CreER and Lyve1-Cre for microglia versus perivascular macrophages; clodronate liposomes and CSF1R inhibitors PLX5622/PLX3397 for depletion; Dbh-Cre and Net-Cre plus GRAB-NE for the noradrenergic readout.
