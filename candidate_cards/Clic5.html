Function in MOp6b: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
CLIC5 belongs to the chloride intracellular channel family, metamorphic proteins that switch between a soluble glutaredoxin-like fold and a membrane-inserted form. Rather than behaving as classical channels, CLIC5A scaffolds ezrin/radixin/moesin and actin at specialised membrane domains (hair-cell stereocilia, podocyte foot processes, cilia), and recent work shows CLIC5 can act as a membrane fusogen.
Region function
Layer 6b is the thin deepest neocortical layer, the surviving remnant of the subplate; it is the only cortical layer directly excited by orexin/hypocretin and projects to higher-order thalamus and across cortex, giving it disproportionate control over brain state and cortical oscillations.
Published in this region
expression only No region-specific literature found. Clic5 appears among deep-layer-6/6b enriched transcripts in cortical cell-type surveys (Kim 2025 consensus definition of deep layer 6 excitatory neurons), but no study has examined CLIC5 protein or function in layer 6b; its characterised roles are in stereocilia, podocytes and cilia (Ott 2023).
Predicted function
Prediction: in layer 6b neurons CLIC5 most plausibly works as an actin-ERM membrane scaffold that stabilises the long apical dendrite and its tuft in layer 1, the compartment through which layer 6b amplifies cortical activation. Conditional deletion (Nxph4-CreER or Ctgf-dgCre crossed to a Clic5 floxed allele) should coarsen apical dendritic morphology and spine density and weaken orexin-driven, layer-6b-dependent brain-state transitions and EEG desynchronisation, rather than change intrinsic chloride currents.
Innovation
★★★★★ 5/5 Layer 6b has only recently become experimentally tractable and no one has tested any CLIC family member there.
Tools
Layer 6b driver lines Ctgf-2A-dgCre and Nxph4-2A-CreER (Allen/JAX); Clic5 mutant mouse alleles (jitterbug); anti-CLIC5 antibodies; no selective pharmacology.
