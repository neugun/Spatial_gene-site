Function in VII: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Gm14496 (NCBI Gene 672125, mouse chromosome 2, six exons) is an uncharacterised predicted gene annotated only by sequence homology as a G protein-coupled receptor, with predicted GPCR signalling activity and plasma-membrane localisation. No ligand, signalling partner or protein-level validation has been published, and it has no established human ortholog.
Region function
The facial motor nucleus (VII) contains the cholinergic motoneurons for the muscles of facial expression, including the mystacial pad muscles that move the whiskers, and drives whisking and blinking.
Published in this region
none No region-specific literature found. There is no published experimental work on Gm14496 in any tissue, only Alliance of Genome Resources electronic annotation predicting G protein-coupled receptor activity.
Predicted function
Prediction: if Gm14496 protein is genuinely made in facial motoneurons, the most plausible role is a neuromodulatory GPCR setting motoneuron excitability or trophic state, as for other orphan GPCRs in cholinergic motoneurons. Without a ligand the phenotype is hard to forecast; a knockout would be most informative assayed on whisking kinematics, blink reflex and facial-nerve regeneration. Expression validation comes first, since such loci are often misannotated.
