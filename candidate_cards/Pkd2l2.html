Function in SCO: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Pkd2l2 encodes polycystin-2-like 2 (TRPP5), a six-transmembrane TRPP-family subunit related to polycystin-2 (PKD2) and PKD2L1. Unlike its relatives it has no established conductance in heterologous systems and its expression is largely restricted to testis and male germ cells. By analogy with other polycystins it is thought to act as a calcium-permeable channel, or a channel partner, in ciliary membranes.
Region function
The subcommissural organ is a tiny ependymal secretory gland at the entrance of the cerebral aqueduct beneath the posterior commissure; it releases SCO-spondin into the CSF, where it condenses into Reissner's fibre, and its failure causes aqueductal stenosis and hydrocephalus.
Published in this region
inferred No region-specific literature found. The closest functional analogue is the paralogue PKD2L1, the defining marker of CSF-contacting neurons lining the central canal and ventricles and of sour-taste cells; PKD2L2 itself has only been examined in germ cells, alongside the related germ-cell polycystin PKDREJ (Sutton 2006).
Predicted function
Prediction: by analogy with PKD2L1 in CSF-contacting neurons and polycystin-2 in renal primary cilia, PKD2L2 in the SCO would most plausibly sit in the apical cilia of ependymocytes as a calcium-permeable, flow- or pH-sensitive channel, letting the gland sense CSF movement and acidity and adjust SCO-spondin secretion accordingly. Knockout is predicted to give no behavioural phenotype but to blunt flow-evoked calcium transients in SCO cilia and mildly destabilise Reissner's-fibre output. Because this is otherwise a testis gene, RNAscope validation should precede any manipulation.
Innovation
★★★★☆ 4/5 A polycystin at the CSF-sensing surface of the subcommissural organ is an untouched idea, but the absence of any Pkd2l2-specific reagent makes it harder than the SCO's other candidates.
Tools
None known for Pkd2l2 itself: no knockout, Cre line, selective blocker or validated antibody in common use. The closest tool set is the PKD2L1 reagents (Pkd2l1-Cre, Pkd2l1 knockout) used for CSF-contacting neurons, plus FoxJ1-CreER (JAX 027012) for generic ependymal manipulation.
