Function in MEPO: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Best1 encodes bestrophin-1, a calcium-activated anion channel whose human mutations cause Best vitelliform macular dystrophy. In brain it is predominantly astrocytic and unusually permeable not only to chloride but to glutamate and GABA, making it a channel-mediated gliotransmitter release route. It supplies the tonic GABA that activates extrasynaptic GABA-A receptors, and reactive astrocytes redistribute it to release excess GABA.
Region function
The median preoptic nucleus is the integrative node of the lamina terminalis, combining osmotic, angiotensin and baroreceptor signals to drive thirst and vasopressin release, and also controlling thermoregulatory heat loss, sleep initiation and cardiovascular set-point.
Published in this region
inferred No region-specific literature found. The closest evidence is Pandit 2015, showing astroglial GABA uptake and tonic GABA-A inhibition regulate presympathetic hypothalamic paraventricular neurons, and Kwak 2020, showing astrocytic Best1-mediated tonic GABA sets sensory acuity in thalamus; Allen ISH gives MEPO 0.088 versus 0.126 for grey matter (experiment 70302992), consistent with diffuse astrocytic expression.
Predicted function
Prediction: Best1 in MEPO is an astrocytic tonic GABA conduit clamping the excitability of glutamatergic thirst- and heat-loss-promoting neurons, setting the threshold at which osmotic and thermal drive becomes drinking or vasomotor heat loss. Astrocyte-specific deletion should lower that threshold, giving exaggerated drinking to modest osmotic challenge, blunted febrile responses or fragmented NREM sleep; overexpression should dampen them.
Innovation
★★★★☆ 4/5 Astrocytic tonic GABA is mapped in hippocampus, cerebellum and thalamus, never in the lamina terminalis.
