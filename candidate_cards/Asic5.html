Function in DCO: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
ASIC5 (also called BLINaC or BASIC) is the most divergent member of the DEG/ENaC acid-sensing ion channel family. Unlike other ASICs it is barely activated by protons; it carries a constitutive Na+-permeable leak current that is gated by bile acids and by changes in membrane mechanics or lipid environment. In neurons such a standing cation conductance sets resting potential and intrinsic excitability.
Region function
The dorsal cochlear nucleus is a cerebellum-like auditory structure that combines primary auditory-nerve input with multimodal somatosensory and vestibular input via a granule-cell parallel fibre system, supporting spectral cue processing for sound localisation and implicated in tinnitus.
Published in this region
expression only Boiko et al. 2014 (PLoS One) mapped Asic5 with a lacZ reporter and found expression essentially restricted in brain to vestibulocerebellar unipolar brush cells plus staining in the dorsal cochlear nucleus, without further characterising the DCO cells. Kreko-Pierce et al. 2020 (Sci Rep) showed Asic5 knockout reduces the intrinsic excitability of type II (mGluR1alpha+, calretinin-negative) unipolar brush cells and causes ataxia, and note that type II UBCs reside chiefly in the vestibulocerebellum and dorsal cochlear nucleus. No DCO-specific physiology has been published.
Predicted function
Prediction: ASIC5 provides a tonic depolarising Na+ leak in DCO unipolar brush cells, the intrinsic amplifier of the granule-cell/parallel-fibre input layer. Knockout should hyperpolarise these cells, reduce spontaneous firing and blunt the prolonged UBC response to mossy fibre glutamate, degrading multisensory gain control over fusiform cells. Behaviourally this predicts impaired sound-localisation and altered gap-detection/tinnitus-like phenotypes rather than deafness; chemogenetic silencing of Asic5+ DCO cells should reproduce the deficit.
Innovation
★★★★★ 5/5 Only a single passing sentence documents Asic5 in the DCO despite an existing knockout line and a well-defined UBC circuit.
Tools
Asic5 knockout / lacZ reporter mouse (Boiko 2014; Kreko-Pierce 2020); UBC subtype markers mGluR1alpha and calretinin; bile acids as activators and the DEG/ENaC blockers amiloride and diminazene; Allen mouse ISH dataset 69236771. No Asic5-Cre line reported.
