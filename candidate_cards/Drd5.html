Function in PBG: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Drd5 encodes the dopamine D5 receptor, a Gs-coupled D1-like GPCR stimulating adenylyl cyclase and cAMP/PKA signalling. It binds dopamine with roughly ten-fold higher affinity than D1 and is constitutively active, so it is thought to mediate tonic rather than phasic dopamine signalling, with a distribution distinct from D1.
Region function
The parabigeminal nucleus, the mammalian nucleus isthmi homologue, is a cholinergic and glutamatergic midbrain nucleus reciprocally connected with superior colliculus and projecting to central amygdala; it supports visual motion processing, attentional selection and looming-evoked defence.
Published in this region
inferred No region-specific literature found for D5 itself. Maltais et al. 2000 reported the parabigeminal nucleus as one of few rat regions with neurons double-labelled for D1 mRNA and D2 receptor protein, establishing that PBG neurons are dopaminoceptive. Ciliax et al. 2000 mapped D5 immunoreactivity broadly across midbrain and brainstem including superior colliculus neuropil, and Khan et al. 2000 found D5 on striatal cholinergic interneurons, the cell class PBG neurons most resemble.
Predicted function
Prediction: D5 in PBG cholinergic neurons provides high-affinity, partly constitutive Gs/cAMP drive setting spontaneous pacemaking rate and thereby scaling cholinergic gain in superficial superior colliculus. Since these neurons fire tonically via an I(A)-dependent mechanism (Goddard et al. 2007), D1-like agonism should accelerate firing, increase collicular acetylcholine release and lower escape thresholds, while Drd5 deletion or local antagonism should slow PBG firing and blunt SC-PBG-driven defensive responses.
Innovation
★★★★★ 5/5 Dopaminergic modulation of the parabigeminal nucleus is unexplored even though the nucleus has a defined defensive circuit and ChAT-Cre access.
Tools
No D5-selective ligand exists, so combine D1-like agonist SKF-81297 and antagonist SCH-23390 with Drd5 knockout or conditional alleles (MGI/IMPC); D5 antibodies validated by Ciliax et al. 2000; PBG cholinergic neurons targetable with ChAT-IRES-Cre (JAX 006410) plus Cre-dependent AAV as in Tokuoka et al. 2020.
