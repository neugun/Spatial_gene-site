Function in IPR: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Chrna2 encodes the alpha-2 subunit of neuronal nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, a ligand-gated cation channel subunit that assembles with beta subunits (mainly beta-2, also beta-4) into pentamers. Binding acetylcholine or nicotine opens a channel permeable to Na+, K+ and Ca2+, depolarizing the neuron and enhancing transmitter release. It is the most restricted alpha subunit in rodent brain.
Region function
The rostral interpeduncular subnucleus receives dense cholinergic and substance-P input from the medial habenula and, through GABAergic and glutamatergic output neurons, controls nicotine aversion, somatic withdrawal signs and anxiety-like states.
Published in this region
direct Chrna2 mRNA is most abundant in the IPN of all rodent brain regions, across subnuclei including the rostral part, with only scattered expression elsewhere (Lotfipour et al. 2013). The same study showed Chrna2 null mice have potentiated nicotine self-administration and altered withdrawal behaviour, and follow-up c-Fos mapping in alpha-2 null mice showed abnormal activity in IPN and connected midbrain/limbic regions during nicotine withdrawal, linking the IPN alpha-2 population directly to withdrawal expression.
Predicted function
Prediction: Alpha-2-containing nAChRs on IPR neurons convert habenular cholinergic drive into depolarization and Ca2+ influx, setting the threshold at which nicotine becomes aversive. IPN-restricted Chrna2 deletion should increase nicotine self-administration and blunt context-dependent somatic withdrawal; chemogenetic activation of Chrna2-positive IPR neurons should produce aversion and withdrawal-like signs, while silencing should be reinforcing at high nicotine doses.
Innovation
★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Chrna2 is already tied functionally to the IPN and nicotine withdrawal, so remaining novelty lies in subnucleus- and cell-type-specific dissection of IPR.
Tools
Chrna2 knockout mice (Lotfipour et al. 2013), Chrna2-Cre driver lines crossed to Ai9 for reporter labelling, general nAChR antagonists mecamylamine and DHbetaE and agonists nicotine/cytisine (no alpha-2-selective small molecule exists), IPN-targeted AAV-Cre or AAV-DIO chemogenetics.
