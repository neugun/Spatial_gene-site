Function in ME: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Tshr encodes the thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor, a leucine-rich-repeat class-A GPCR with a large hormone-binding ectodomain. TSH binding activates Gs-cAMP-PKA and, at higher occupancy, Gq-PLC. Outside the thyroid, TSHR on hypothalamic tanycytes reads TSH released locally from the pituitary pars tuberalis.
Region function
The median eminence and adjacent pars tuberalis form the neurohaemal hub where tanycytes, portal capillaries and neurosecretory terminals meet; tanycytes locally convert thyroid hormone and gate GnRH and TRH access to portal blood.
Published in this region
direct Direct and well developed. Ono et al. 2008 (PNAS) showed long photoperiod induces pars tuberalis Tshb and that TSH acting on TSHR in the mediobasal hypothalamus/median eminence induces Dio2 and suppresses Dio3, raising local T3. Bolborea et al. 2015 (J Mol Endocrinol) showed tanycyte TSHR signals via both cAMP and Ca2+/PKC. Kameda 2023 (J Comp Neurol) documented photoperiod-dependent tanycyte and pars tuberalis changes; Rodriguez-Rodriguez et al. 2019 reviewed tanycyte control of TRH flux into portal capillaries.
Predicted function
Prediction: TSHR converts the melatonin-decoded photoperiod signal into intra-hypothalamic T3 by switching the tanycyte Dio2/Dio3 balance, remodelling end-feet around GnRH terminals and gating seasonality. Tanycyte-selective Tshr deletion should abolish long-day Dio2 induction and block photoperiodic gonadal activation; local TSH infusion or tanycyte Gs-DREADD activation should mimic a summer state with raised T3 and gonadal recrudescence.
Innovation
★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 TSHR signalling in tanycytes of the median eminence is the textbook mechanism of mammalian photoperiodism and has been studied for nearly two decades.
Tools
Tshr knockout and hyt/hyt mice; floxed Tshr alleles; tanycyte drivers Rax-CreERT2 and Dio2/Gpr50 promoters with ICV AAV1; recombinant bovine TSH for ICV infusion; anti-TSHR antibodies; TSHR small-molecule agonists/antagonists.
