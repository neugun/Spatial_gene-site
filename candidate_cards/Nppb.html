Function in DMX: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Nppb encodes B-type (brain) natriuretic peptide, a secreted peptide hormone of the natriuretic peptide family released mainly by cardiac myocytes under wall stress. BNP binds the receptor guanylyl cyclase NPR-A (Npr1), raising cGMP and PKG signalling to promote natriuresis, vasodilation and reduced sympathetic drive; it is cleared by NPR-C. In the nervous system BNP acts as a neuropeptide transmitter, most famously in the spinal itch pathway.
Region function
The dorsal motor nucleus of the vagus supplies the cholinergic preganglionic parasympathetic outflow to the gut, pancreas and heart, controlling gastric motility and secretion, insulin release and vagal tone.
Published in this region
expression only Tao et al. 2021 (Neuron) profiled DMV motor neurons by single-cell RNA-seq and defined seven subtypes, one of which is marked by Nppb, with subtype-specific Cre drivers used to show labelled-line brain-to-gut control of the glandular stomach. Hornung et al. 2024 (iScience) mapped cardiac-projecting DMV neurons and explicitly aligned their transcriptomic subtype 2 to the 'Nppb and Trpv1 neurons from Tao et al.' No study has manipulated Nppb itself in the DMV.
Predicted function
Prediction: BNP is a co-transmitter released alongside acetylcholine by one labelled-line class of vagal preganglionic neurons, acting on NPR-A/cGMP in enteric or cardiac ganglion targets to slow and smooth cholinergic drive. Conditional Nppb deletion from DMV neurons should leave gross vagal tone intact but alter gastric secretion/accommodation or heart-rate variability; chemogenetic activation of Nppb+ DMV neurons should produce a selective visceral output distinct from Cck+ or Pdyn+ DMV lines, and NPR-A blockade in the target organ should occlude it.
Innovation
★★★★☆ 4/5 Nppb is established as a DMV subtype marker but its peptide function in vagal output has never been tested.
Tools
Nppb-Cre and Nppb knockout mice (used in the itch field); Chat-Cre and Phox2b drivers for vagal motor neurons; Npr1 (NPR-A) knockout and the antagonist A-71915; anti-proBNP antibodies and BNP RIA/ELISA; Allen mouse ISH dataset 69174475.
