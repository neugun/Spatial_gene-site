Function in OV: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
SCTR is the class B (secretin/glucagon family) Gs-coupled GPCR for secretin, raising cAMP and PKA to drive pancreatic and biliary bicarbonate secretion. Centrally the secretin-SCTR axis amplifies angiotensin II signalling, promoting water intake and vasopressin expression and release.
Region function
The OVLT, a lamina terminalis circumventricular organ, detects plasma hyperosmolality and angiotensin II, relaying to MnPO, SON and PVH for thirst and vasopressin secretion.
Published in this region
expression only Lee et al. 2010: secretin and SCTR transcript and protein throughout the lamina terminalis, including OVLT; Sct-/- and Sctr-/- mice lose most ANGII-evoked drinking and vasopressin responses. Chu et al. 2009: secretin is a neurohypophysial factor acting on SON/PVH. Zhang et al. 2022 deleted Sctr in adjacent SFO, cutting water but not salt intake in dehydrated mice; secretin evoked inward currents in SFO nNOS neurons.
Predicted function
Prediction: OVLT SCTR mirrors SFO SCTR, a Gs-coupled amplifier of glutamatergic/nNOS osmosensory excitability. AAV-Cre deletion of floxed Sctr should blunt dehydration- and ANGII-induced drinking and reduce SON/PVH Fos, sparing sodium appetite; local secretin should be dipsogenic and preferentially osmotic rather than volumetric.
Innovation
★★★☆☆ 3/5 The secretin-SCTR thirst axis is established in SFO and magnocellular nuclei; the OVLT node is untested.
Tools
Sctr-/- and Sct-/- global knockouts; floxed Sctr plus AAV-Cre for region-restricted deletion (Zhang et al. 2022); secretin agonist; Nos1-Cre and Slc17a6-Cre for lamina terminalis osmosensory neurons.
