Supported claim

Functional readability is layered and dataset-specific; continuous molecular state can add information below identity, while other systems are dominated by space or structural coordinates.

Boundary / not claimed

No universal 'genes always win' claim; session-held MICRONS is not cross-animal molecular evidence; different metrics are not collapsed into one score.
