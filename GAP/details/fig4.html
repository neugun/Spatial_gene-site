Ask whether molecular coordinates organize a functional manifold and whether they add unique information about an omitted behavior after other functions are known.

Supported claim

C. elegans, Xu, Bugeon, Sorensen, WARP and Allen VC2P establish multiple forms of molecularly organized function; the strongest result depends on headroom and representation.

Boundary / not claimed

A weak global manifold does not erase strong selected-axis completion. Condylis saturation, Zhao native negative completion, sparse-marker Shainer/Habenula and non-molecular NeuroPlex remain boundaries.
