Function in OT: evidence, prediction, innovation

Gene function
Drd3 encodes the dopamine D3 receptor, a Gi/o-coupled D2-like GPCR that inhibits cAMP/PKA signalling and reduces excitability. Higher dopamine affinity than D2 and restricted limbic distribution make it an antipsychotic and addiction target.
Region function
The olfactory tubercle is the olfactory ventral striatum: olfactory bulb and VTA dopamine input, D1/D2 spiny neurons, islands of Calleja, encoding odour valence and reward.
Published in this region
direct OT islands of Calleja granule cells are defined by D3 expression (Ridray et al. 1998: D1/D3 coexpression, opposing and synergistic, in islands and accumbens shell). Zhang et al. 2021: optogenetic activation of OT D3 neurons initiates self-grooming and suppresses ongoing behaviour; inhibition halts and ablation reduces grooming. Zhang et al. 2023: restraint stress lowers OT D3 excitability; ablation or inhibition gives depression-like behaviour, activation rescues it.
Predicted function
Prediction: D3 on island granule cells is an autoinhibitory brake on dopamine-driven excitability; agonists (PD128907) should suppress grooming initiation, antagonists (SB-277011A, PG01037) or OT-restricted Drd3 deletion should increase grooming. Conditional knockouts are needed because existing work manipulates the D3-Cre cell type, not the receptor.
Innovation
★★☆☆☆ 2/5 The D3 island cell type is heavily studied, but the receptor's own signalling role is not genetically isolated.
Tools
Tg(Drd3-cre)KI198Gsat BAC line (MMRRC) for OT islands, crossed with Ai9 tdTomato (JAX 007909) and Ai32 ChR2 (JAX 024109); Drd3 knockouts; agonists PD128907, 7-OH-DPAT; antagonists SB-277011A, PG01037, nafadotride; anti-D3 antibodies.
