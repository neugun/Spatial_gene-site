Spatial_gene

Redirecting to the gene evidence report.

  • Region explorer and 3-D brain - type any of the 554 CCF regions to list the genes confined to it; the transparent brain shows every region that has strict-exclusive genes
  • The Bridge from Molecular to Systems Neuroscience - 286 shortlisted genes with spatial tier, top region, published role, editable experimental plan
  • Screening report - pipeline QC, priority genes, ranked single genes, intersections
  • Gene cards (308) and priority cards (5)
  • Tables: final_priority_shortlist.csv, strict_exclusive_candidates.csv, region_universe.csv, run_manifest.json

Source code and methods: github.com/neugun/Spatial_gene (private). Zhenggang Zhu, 2026.
