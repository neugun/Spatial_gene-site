Route B — Stage935–938 Slc17a6 optimization

Route B has moved beyond the old single Stage916/917 detector gate. Stage935 systematically swept 12 frozen-RS-FISH detector settings on the same Slc17a6 S530 64-plane full-XY slab. The best stability/grid compromise was sigma=1.65, threshold=0.0025: detector grid L2 ratio 0.988, raw/corrected spot matching 97.5%/97.7%, and coarse spatial correlation 0.999656.

Stage936 then swept image-gain strength while keeping the two best detector regimes. Stronger gain improves detector-grid suppression but increases spot turnover, so Stage937 mapped all ten gain×detector candidates back to the same cell universe and compared them directly with current Stage934 Route A.

Current best slab candidate: gain alpha=1.5, stable detector (sigma=1.65, threshold=0.0025). It gives Route-A convergence mean 0.175; y-delta profile correlation rises to 0.246, but x-delta correlation remains -0.110. The slab cell-power ratio is still 0.986 versus the full-section Stage934 target 0.441, so this is not yet promoted as a production Route B authority.

Stage938 result: the best candidate removes the column gain component entirely (row=1.0, column=0.0), which flips the x-delta correlation back positive while retaining y convergence. The remaining limitation is coverage/magnitude: the 64-plane slab still does not approach the full-section Stage934 power target.
